0.1.0

6 November 2026

The first version for everyone.

  • Keep your agent. Claude Code, Codex, Grok or any command runs in its real terminal, with your config and subscription.
  • Files, diffs and pages are tabs beside the terminal, not in a drawer.
  • ⌘-click any path. The file floats over the session, and a path that doesn't exist as written still opens.
  • Every agent at a glance. The one that needs you is marked.
  • It improves itself. Ask, and your agent rewrites Tiri. Try it, then keep it or go back.
  • Your servers over your own SSH config.
  • Agents outlive the app. A quit, crash or force quit loses nothing, unsaved edits included.
  • Themes for dark and light, or bring one from Cursor, VS Code or Ghostty.
  • A window per workspace, each with its own files and terminals.
  • English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
  • VoiceOver, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion.
  • Collects nothing. Turn off update checks, and Tiri never goes online by itself.
