What's new in Tiri.

Newest first. Tiri updates itself, and your changes stay through every update.

0.2.0

7 November 2026

Faster terminals, and files from Finder.

  • Terminals draw on the graphics chip. Heavy output no longer holds up the rest of Tiri.
  • Files from Finder. Copy them in Finder and paste them on a folder in the explorer, or drop them on it, on your Mac or on a server. ⌘C in the explorer copies files back out.
  • ⌘-click any part of a long path, even one an agent wrapped over several lines.
  • ⌘-click finds files git ignores, like screenshots, logs and build output, and never opens another file on the same line.
  • Codex's first turn shows what it changed.
  • Several cursors: ⌘/, indent, and moving or deleting lines work at every cursor.
  • Smaller fixes. A deleted file no longer shows up in quick open. A ⌘-clicked folder shows what's inside. Review comments keep quotes and dashes as you typed them. Jumping to the end of a long diff is immediate.

0.1.0

6 November 2026

The first version for everyone.

  • Keep your agent. Claude Code, Codex, Grok or any command runs in its real terminal, with your config and subscription.
  • Files, diffs and pages are tabs beside the terminal, not in a drawer.
  • ⌘-click any path. The file floats over the session, and a path that doesn't exist as written still opens.
  • Every agent at a glance. The one that needs you is marked.
  • It improves itself. Ask, and your agent rewrites Tiri. Try it, then keep it or go back.
  • Your servers over your own SSH config.
  • Agents outlive the app. A quit, crash or force quit loses nothing, unsaved edits included.
  • Hard to stall. A huge paste, an agent rewriting an open file, a lid closed on an SSH session or a page that crashes stays in its own tab.
  • Themes for dark and light, or bring one from Cursor, VS Code or Ghostty.
  • A window per workspace, each with its own files and terminals.
  • English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
  • VoiceOver, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion.
  • Collects nothing. Turn off update checks, and Tiri never goes online by itself.
